Workflow

Keep restoration jobs from disappearing between intake and follow-up.

Restoration job management often spans urgent calls, crew availability, documentation handoffs, equipment checks, estimates, and return visits. Gainhelm focuses on the dispatch and scheduling layer so small teams can keep the next step clear.

Centralize job intake

Capture the address, loss type, priority, access notes, customer updates, and who needs to be assigned before the job turns into a text-thread scramble.

Schedule crews and follow-up

Keep crew scheduling, drying checks, equipment pickup, estimate handoffs, and return visits visible on a readable board.

Connect field and office updates

Give office staff a simple place to see field notes, photos-to-review reminders, job status, and what needs action next.

Stay software-only

Gainhelm helps restoration operators manage work. It does not perform water extraction, mitigation, mold remediation, or emergency repair services.
