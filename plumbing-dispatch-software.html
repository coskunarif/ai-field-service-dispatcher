Plumbing dispatcher script templates for service and emergency calls.

Standard service call script: Best for routine issues (e.g. slow drains, dripping faucets, fixture replacements) where same-day plumber scheduling is not critical.

Dispatcher Greeting

"Thank you for calling [Company Name], my name is [Your Name]. How can we make your plumbing work today?"

Identify the Problem

"I can certainly help you with that. Can you describe the issue you're experiencing with your [drain/faucet/toilet]?"

Pro Tip: Ask when they first noticed it to gauge if it's a slow leak or active problem.
Gather Customer Info

"Let's get your details so we can schedule a plumber. What is the address of the property, and what is the best phone number to reach you?"

Confirm and Schedule

"We have our technician, [Technician Name], available in your area this [morning/afternoon] between [Time Window]. I'll book that window for you, and we will text you when they are on their way."

Emergency plumbing script: Best for high-urgency burst pipes, active sewer backups, or flooding. Focuses on damage control and fast dispatcher routing.

Urgent Greeting

"Thank you for calling [Company Name] Emergency Services, this is [Your Name]. What is your plumbing emergency?"

Assess and Instruct

"I understand this is urgent. If there is active flooding, do you know where the main water shut-off valve is located? Please turn it off immediately to minimize water damage while I dispatch a plumber."

Pro Tip: Guiding customers to shut off water is a crucial step in a burst pipe service dispatch workflow.
Log Urgent Details

"What is the exact street address for the emergency? I am updating our team live as we speak."

Immediate Dispatch Booking

"I have locked in our emergency plumber, [Technician Name]. They are wrapping up their current job and are routing to you next. Their estimated arrival is within [Time Window]. I will send a text notification the second they head your way."

💡 How Gainhelm simplifies this: Instead of scribbling call details on paper and scrambling to find who is available, type these details directly into your Gainhelm board. Gainhelm automatically suggests the right plumber based on current proximity and availability, helping you run a seamless emergency plumbing dispatch workflow.
