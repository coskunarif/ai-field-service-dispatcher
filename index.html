How it works

Three steps to a cleaner dispatch flow

Add the job

Capture the customer request, location, and work details in seconds. AI extracts key info so you don't have to type everything.

Track progress in real time. Techs update status from the field, customers get notified, and nothing falls through the cracks.

FAQ

Questions people ask before joining

What is Gainhelm?

Gainhelm is AI dispatch app and software for field service teams. It is built to help HVAC, plumbing, and landscaping businesses keep jobs organized and moving from intake to completion.

Who should use Gainhelm?

Gainhelm is for small and growing service businesses that want a clearer dispatch workflow without adopting a heavy enterprise system. If you run 3 to 50 technicians, Gainhelm is built for you.

Is Gainhelm available right now?

Gainhelm is collecting waitlist signups while early access continues to roll out. Join the waitlist to be among the first to get access when we open to new teams.

How do I get early access?

What does Gainhelm replace?

Gainhelm is designed to replace messy spreadsheets, text threads, whiteboards, and ad hoc handoff workflows with one clear dispatch board that your whole team can use.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Technicians get a dedicated mobile app to view their schedule, update job status, and communicate with the office. The dispatch dashboard works on any desktop or tablet browser.
