Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about contractor dispatch ROI.

How is dispatch phone tag cost calculated for trade contractors?

Dispatch phone tag cost is calculated by multiplying total daily technician service calls by average minutes lost per dispatch (typically 15 to 25 minutes of back-and-forth calls), converted into hourly labor and office overhead costs over 22 work days per month.

How does app-less SMS dispatching recover lost contractor revenue?

By eliminating phone tag and manual schedule drag-and-drop, automated SMS dispatching lets field technicians accept jobs in one tap via standard text. This saves 2 to 3 hours daily per crew, unlocking capacity for 1 to 2 extra billable jobs each week.
