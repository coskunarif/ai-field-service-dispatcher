How to automate after-hours plumbing dispatch.

Gainhelm is a lightweight plumbing dispatch software and plumber scheduling app built for small plumbing teams to coordinate technician assignments, manage emergency calls, and automate after-hours dispatches via SMS text messaging without app setup friction.

Instant access to the interactive SMS simulator. We’ll only use this to follow up about early access.
Eliminate phone tag on emergency plumbing calls. Dispatch on-call technicians instantly via simple SMS alerts.

1. Instant Call & Job Intake

When an emergency water leak or drain backup call arrives after hours, Gainhelm captures job details, location, and urgency in real time.

2. Smart On-Call Technician Lookup

The dispatch engine checks on-call roster schedules and automatically identifies the primary plumber assigned to night shift duty.

3. App-Less SMS Dispatching

A structured text message is sent to the on-call plumber with job address, issue details, and simple quick-reply actions.

4. Escalation & Calendar Sync

If unconfirmed within 5 minutes, Gainhelm automatically routes the job to the secondary plumber and updates Google Calendar instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does after-hours plumbing dispatch work without an app?

Gainhelm routes incoming emergency plumbing calls to on-call plumbers via simple SMS text messaging so technicians do not need to log into complex mobile apps.

What happens if an on-call plumber does not reply after hours?

If the primary on-call plumber does not confirm the SMS dispatch within 5 minutes, Gainhelm automatically escalates and routes the job to the secondary on-call plumber.

Can emergency plumbing dispatches sync with Google Calendar?

Yes. Once an after-hours emergency plumbing job is accepted, it automatically syncs to the dispatcher and technician Google Calendar schedules in real time.
