# Gainhelm promotion brief

Date: 2026-05-12

## Evidence-backed positioning

Gainhelm is an AI dispatch app and software for small field service teams. The public homepage positions it as a way to turn service calls into assigned jobs without the dispatch scramble, helping HVAC, plumbing, and landscaping teams see open jobs, match jobs to technicians, and keep office/field teams aligned from intake to arrival.

Observed site evidence:
- Homepage title: `Gainhelm | AI Dispatch App & Software for Field Service Teams`
- Meta description: `Gainhelm is an AI dispatch app and software for field service teams. Schedule jobs faster, reduce phone tag, and keep HVAC, plumbing, and landscaping work moving.`
- Primary CTA: `Get early access`
- Early-access signals: `Built for 1–20 tech teams`, `No credit card needed`
- Core pain: phone tag, spreadsheet schedules, manual handoffs, scattered notes, callback loops
- Core audiences: HVAC teams, plumbing companies, landscaping crews; especially owners and office managers running dispatch through calls, texts, spreadsheets, and memory
- Existing SEO assets: `/hvac-dispatch-software`, `/plumbing-dispatch-software`, `/field-service-scheduling`, comparison/how-to pages, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`

Keep claims conservative: do not claim paid customer results, production integrations, advanced CRM/payments, or fully built mobile technician workflows unless product evidence is added.

## Audience and message

Primary audience: owners, operators, office managers, and dispatchers at 1–20 technician HVAC, plumbing, and landscaping businesses.

Main message: if dispatch is scattered across texts, calls, spreadsheets, and memory, Gainhelm gives you a cleaner AI-assisted dispatch workflow so jobs move from intake to assignment faster.

Best CTA: join the early-access waitlist at `https://gainhelm.com/?utm_source={platform}&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=early_access_launch`.
