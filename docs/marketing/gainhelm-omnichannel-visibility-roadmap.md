# Gainhelm Omnichannel Visibility Strategy & Promotion Roadmap

This roadmap establishes a comprehensive, $0-ad-budget distribution plan to maximize Gainhelm's visibility across high-intent directories, niche trade communities, review platforms, and search engines (traditional + AI).

---

## 📋 Strategic Posture: Early-Access & Value-First
To prevent spam bans and build brand trust, Gainhelm’s promotion uses two primary postures:
1. **Directories**: High-utility listings highlighting early-access, clean UI, and focus on small 1–20 tech teams.
2. **Communities**: Problem-first engagement focusing on dispatch scheduling pain (phone tag, spreadsheet limits) and seeking operator feedback rather than direct pitches.

#### 📝 Reddit Value-First Copy Template
```text
Title: How are small HVAC/plumbing teams handling dispatch once spreadsheets get messy?

I’ve been interviewing small trade business owners and office managers (mainly 1-20 tech crews) about their scheduling workflows. 

Almost everyone starts with the same stack: calls, texts, a whiteboard/spreadsheet, and one manager’s memory. That works great when you have 2-3 techs. But once you hit 5-10 techs, things seem to break down.

If you run or work in a trade business, what is the single hardest part of dispatch for you?
1. Matching the right technician’s skills/location to the job?
2. Rescheduling emergency service calls on a packed day?
3. The endless phone tag and texting to verify a tech got the job details?
4. Keeping customer callbacks and ETA updates moving?

I'm building an early-access tool to make this flow a bit cleaner, but I want to make sure I'm solving real headaches rather than building features nobody cares about. I'd love to hear your experiences or horror stories!
```

## 🤖 Tier 5: Programmatic SEO & AI Search Engine Optimization (LLMO/GEO)
Traditional search engines and AI search assistants (Perplexity, Gemini, SearchGPT) account for a significant portion of B2B purchase discovery.

### 1. AI Search Citation (Generative Engine Optimization)
* **Direct Answers**: Structure landing pages to answer core questions directly in the first 40–60 words (AI bots extract the top sentences).
* **FAQ & Software Schema**: Integrate comprehensive `JSON-LD` schemas on every page:
  * `SoftwareApplication` (defining application category, Operating System, pricing model)
  * `FAQPage` (answering key user questions)
* **Maintain the `llms.txt`**: Ensure `/llms.txt` is updated with high-salience terms (e.g. `HVAC dispatch app`, `plumbing scheduling software`) and direct links to landing pages so crawlers index them easily.

### 2. Trade & Geo-Targeted Programmatic Landing Pages
Expand Gainhelm's existing trade landing pages into hyper-targeted long-tail keywords matching buyer searches:
* **Competitor Comparison Pages**:
  * `/gainhelm-vs-jobber`
  * `/gainhelm-vs-housecall-pro`
  * `/gainhelm-vs-servicetitan` (targeted at teams that outgrew spreadsheets but find ServiceTitan too bloated/expensive)
* **Geo-Targeted Content Clusters**:
  * Create pages answering: "Simple scheduling software for HVAC teams in Texas" or "Best plumbing dispatch app Atlanta".
