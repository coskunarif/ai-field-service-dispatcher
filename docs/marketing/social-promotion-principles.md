# Social promotion principles for Gainhelm

Date: 2026-05-12

Use these principles for future Reddit, Facebook, and community promotion.

## Principles

1. Prefer one relevant community at a time over broad posting. Avoid mass posting, DMs, vote/react manipulation, or repeated copy-paste.
2. Read destination rules first. If self-promotion, market research, or links are banned, do not post. Use comments only when genuinely helpful and relevant.
3. Lead with the operator problem, not the product. For Gainhelm, the safest angle is dispatch workflow research for small HVAC, plumbing, and field-service teams.
4. Make the post feedback-oriented: disclose that Gainhelm is an early-access tool, ask what breaks in current dispatch workflows, and avoid pretending to be a neutral third party.
5. Keep claims conservative. Do not claim customer results, integrations, route optimization, payments, CRM, or production traction unless there is evidence.
6. Use UTMs on links so any traffic can be attributed, but do not claim traction until analytics or platform engagement proves it.
7. Respect session/auth boundaries. Reddit uses docker-chrome-3; Facebook uses docker-chrome-4. Recheck auth immediately before posting because sessions can expire.
8. Capture evidence immediately after posting: permalink, post detail output, screenshot, platform confirmation, or clear blocker/pending-moderation evidence.
9. If a Facebook group post returns success but no permalink is surfaced, report it as submitted/unverified rather than live promotion with a URL.
10. Monitor lightly after posting for comments or removals; reply with useful context, not hard selling.

## Current approved copy style

For Reddit, use a value-first research post such as: "How are small HVAC teams handling dispatch once spreadsheets get messy?" Ask about assignment, schedule clarity, callbacks, emergencies, and technician context, then add the Gainhelm early-access link only for context if rules allow it.

For Facebook groups, use a short contractor-focused question: "For small plumbing contractors: how are you handling daily dispatch right now?" Explain that Gainhelm is early-access and ask what the hardest dispatch problem is.
