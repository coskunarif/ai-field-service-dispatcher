FAQ

Common questions about choosing a lightweight alternative.

Compare dispatch software features before making a choice for your field service team.

When is FieldEdge the right choice?

FieldEdge is great if you have a large team (20+ trucks), need deep built-in inventory tracking, advanced marketing automation tools, and heavy back-office accounting integrations.

Why is Gainhelm better for smaller teams?

Small teams don't have dedicated IT staff or hours to configure software. Gainhelm handles incoming jobs, technician routing, and mobile schedule handoffs in a simple interface that anyone can learn in 5 minutes.

Can I import my data?

Yes. Gainhelm is designed to integrate with simple customer CSVs so you can move your client database and technician lists over without manual typing.
