How to sync Google Calendar with field tech dispatch.

Gainhelm is a lightweight HVAC and plumbing dispatch software that connects Google Calendar scheduling with app-less SMS text notifications to keep technician assignments and dispatch boards synchronized without manual data entry.

Instant access to the interactive SMS simulator. We’ll only use this to follow up about early access.
Keep your Google Calendar and field service dispatch board in perfect sync automatically.

1. Connect Your Google Calendar iCal Feed

In Gainhelm setup, enter your technician's Google Calendar iCal feed URL to establish instant read/write synchronization.

2. Auto-Generate SMS Tech Notifications

When a dispatch call is assigned or rescheduled, Gainhelm formats job details into clean SMS alerts sent directly to the technician.

3. Real-Time Status Updates

Technicians reply via SMS to accept or update job status, and Gainhelm automatically updates the Google Calendar event color and details.

Why Google Calendar Sync Works

  • Works with iOS, Android, and Web Google Calendars
  • No tech training or complex app downloads
  • Eliminates double-booking and schedule conflicts
  • Instant SMS fallback if tech is offline

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Google Calendar sync with technician SMS dispatch?

Gainhelm connects to Google Calendar iCal feeds so whenever a new job or HVAC/plumbing service call is booked, the technician receives an automated SMS dispatch.

Do field technicians need to install a special Google Calendar app?

No. Field technicians can use their standard native Google Calendar app or receive job updates directly via SMS text message without installing additional software.

Can multiple HVAC and plumbing technician calendars be synced?

Yes. Gainhelm supports multi-technician Google Calendar synchronization for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other field service dispatch boards.
