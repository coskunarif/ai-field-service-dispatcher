# Gainhelm Organic Distribution & Platform Launch Plan

This action plan details the next steps to distribute GainHelm across startup directories, trade forums, and software listicles. Since Gainhelm is in early-access waitlist status, we split the distribution strategy into three high-leverage phases.

---

## Phase 1: High-Priority Directory Submissions (Maker Account Required)

These Crowdsourced and SaaS platforms drive direct, high-intent traffic from users comparing software options. Because they require OAuth login, developer profiles, or CAPTCHA validation, these must be handled by creating a verified brand account:

### 1. AlternativeTo (`https://alternativeto.net`)

- **Objective**: Gain visibility on the comparison pages for our primary competitors.
- **Action Item**:
  1. Register a maker profile using `arif.coskun@profithelm.com`.
  2. Create a product page for GainHelm with the canonical link: `https://gainhelm.com`.
  3. Tag GainHelm as an **alternative** to: **Jobber**, **Housecall Pro**, **ServiceTitan**, and **Service Fusion**.
  4. Link to our corresponding alternative landing pages (e.g., `/jobber-alternative.html`).

### 2. SaaSHub (`https://www.saashub.com`)

- **Objective**: Get listed in crowdsourced software collections and competitor comparisons.
- **Action Item**:
  1. Register a developer profile.
  2. Submit GainHelm under "Field Service Management" and "Scheduling" categories.
  3. Link comparison cards pointing to competitor alternatives.

### 3. BetaList (`https://betalist.com`)

- **Objective**: Tap into early-adopter/tech startup traffic looking to test new waitlists.
- **Action Item**:
  1. Submit GainHelm's pre-launch waitlist page.
  2. Use the free editorial queue (takes 2-4 weeks) or opt for paid fast-track if budget allows.

### 4. Gartner Digital Markets (Capterra / GetApp) & G2

- **Objective**: Establish product authority on major enterprise review directories.
- **Action Item**:
  1. Set up a free vendor profile at `https://www.capterra.com/vendors/` and `https://www.g2.com/products/new`.
  2. Provide business verification info (head-office city, phone, and standard logo).
  3. Once approved on Capterra, request syndication to GetApp and Software Advice.

## Phase 2: Trade Community Forum Engagement (Value-First Strategy)

Trade communities (Reddit, PlumbingZone, HVAC-Talk) are highly protective against direct advertising. To get traffic and backlinks from these forums, we must use the **Zero-Link, Value-First Strategy**:

### 1. Account Setups & Signatures

- **PlumbingZone (`plumbingzone.com`) & HVAC-Talk (`hvac-talk.com`)**:
  - Set up accounts representing yourself as the founder building a simpler, app-less tool for trade owners.
  - Configure a signature line:  
    `Gainhelm | App-Less AI Dispatching for Trade Teams | Google Calendar Sync`
  - Participate in "Business Management" and "Software" categories. Your helpful answers will display the signature link automatically.

### 2. Reddit Engagement (r/HVAC, r/plumbing, r/electricians, r/sweatystartup)

- **Rule**: Keep self-promotion below 10% of total activity. Disclose affiliation.
- **Action Item**:
  - Identify threads discussing scheduling fatigue, text chaos with crews, or competitor pricing frustration.
  - Answer the threads from an operational perspective (e.g., _"We found that field technicians refuse to use heavy calendar apps while driving, so we moved our crew to a hands-free SMS setup..."_).
  - Do not post direct links in the initial post. If users reply asking for the tool name, follow up with the link.
