## Community answer draft — HVAC dispatch software

For a 1–20 tech HVAC team, I’d separate the decision into two buckets: enterprise FSM platforms and lightweight dispatch tools. ServiceTitan/Jobber/Housecall Pro can be strong if you want a broad business system, but some smaller teams mainly need a cleaner dispatch flow: intake, see who is available, assign the right tech, and keep job context out of text threads.

If the pain is mostly dispatch chaos rather than full CRM/accounting replacement, look for: readable dispatch board, fast job intake, technician availability/skills, mobile-friendly updates, and simple follow-through. GainHelm is one option in that lighter-weight category: https://gainhelm.com/hvac-dispatch-software

## Community answer draft — plumbing scheduling/dispatch

For plumbing teams, the biggest scheduling problem is usually not just a calendar; it’s keeping intake, dispatch, tech availability, and customer follow-up in one place. If you’re small, be careful with platforms that are powerful but heavier than what the office can adopt quickly.

A practical checklist: can you capture a call quickly, see which plumber can take it, assign with enough job context, handle emergency changes, and avoid repeated calls/texts? GainHelm is built around that small-team dispatch workflow for plumbing and field-service teams: https://gainhelm.com/plumbing-dispatch-app
