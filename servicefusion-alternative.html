FAQ

Common questions about choosing a lightweight alternative.

Compare dispatch software features before making a choice for your field service team.

When is Service Fusion the right choice?

Service Fusion is a solid fit if you need heavy warehouse parts tracking, built-in fleet GPS hardware integration, multi-stage dispatching processes, or complex back-office administrative control boards.

Why is Gainhelm better for smaller teams?

Owners who manage scheduling alongside other daily operations need a tool that is fast and visual. Gainhelm keeps your board clean and accessible from anywhere without clicking through dozens of setup menus.

Can I import my data?

Yes. Gainhelm is designed to import your existing clients and technician contact details via simple csv files.
