# MISSION.md - Project Mission & North Star Metrics

## Objective
Establish Gainhelm as the leading agent-first, context-driven AI dispatcher for small field service trade contractors by maximizing organic acquisition and streamlining product adoption.

## North Star Metrics
- **Metric 1: Waitlist Conversion Rate (WCR)**
  - **Definition**: The ratio of unique landing page visitors to completed waitlist signups.
  - **Target**: Increase conversion rate from baseline to > 5%.
- **Metric 2: Search Console CTR & Position (CTR-POS)**
  - **Definition**: The click-through rate and average search positions for trade-specific dispatch keywords.
  - **Target**: Average search position < 10 for HVAC, Plumbing, and Electrical scheduling keywords.
- **Metric 3: Setup Wizard Completion Rate (SWCR)**
  - **Definition**: The percentage of onboarded trade users who successfully complete the Context Configuration Wizard (`/setup`).
  - **Target**: Completion rate > 80%.

## Active Focus Areas
- **SEO/GEO Polish**: Optimizing static HTML landing pages and JSON-LD metadata for organic search and AI search engine citation (LLMO/GEO).
- **Adoption Flow Optimization**: Lowering setup configuration friction for new trade templates.
- **Agent Scheduling Latency & Precision**: Improving accuracy and response speed of dispatch notifications.
