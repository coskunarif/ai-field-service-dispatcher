# FieldServiceAI — App Description

## What It Does
An AI-powered dispatch tool that automatically assigns the right technician to each job based on location, skills, and availability — instead of owners doing it manually by phone.

## Who Uses It
Small HVAC, plumbing, and landscaping businesses (1-20 technicians). Owners who waste 3+ hours daily shuffling schedules.

## How You Make Money
**SaaS subscription model** — charge per team member per month ($49-$200/mo typical for this market).

- They pay to save time on scheduling
- They pay to invoice faster and get paid faster
- They pay because their competitors already have something like this

## The Problem It Solves
Field service owners hate daily dispatching — it's repetitive, time-consuming, and causes phone tag.

## The Solution
AI automatically matches jobs to the right technicians. Owners stop doing it manually.

## Current Status
Prototype landing page live with waitlist collecting emails via the API backend and storing leads in Postgres.

## Philosophy
We use SEO and GEO as a listening system before building product features.
Landing pages are probes for demand, not just marketing pages.
Search Console is the signal source: if users repeatedly search for a problem, that problem earns the next build.

## What We Want To Build
A simple dispatch product that reduces phone tag, keeps scheduling clear, and helps small field service teams work faster without spreadsheet chaos.

## Rules Of The Map
- one page = one intent
- one intent = one clear problem
- build only after signal repeats
- keep the first screen simple and readable
- prefer clarity over feature bloat
