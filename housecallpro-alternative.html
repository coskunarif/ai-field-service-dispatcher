Common questions about choosing a lightweight alternative.

Compare dispatch software features before making a choice for your field service team.

When is Housecall Pro the right choice?

Housecall Pro is a great choice if you want to offer consumer financing on jobs, send physical postcards to past clients, automate customer feedback surveys, or process mobile credit cards directly inside the field app.

Why is Gainhelm better for smaller teams?

If your main operational bottleneck is scheduling calls and assigning technicians, Gainhelm gives you a fast, dedicated tool without forcing you to click through multiple CRM screens.

Can I import my data?

Yes. Gainhelm supports simple spreadsheet import for client and technician lists.
