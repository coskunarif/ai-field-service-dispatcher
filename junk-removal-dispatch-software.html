🚀 Agent-First Dispatching

Instead of managing messy visual calendars, Gainhelm runs a headless AI dispatcher. Technicians coordinate jobs entirely via automated text messages, while you supervise real-time logs and business rules from a single dashboard.

Workflow

Keep junk removal jobs moving from intake to assignment.

Junk removal dispatch can break down when volume estimates, location access, job notes, and schedule changes live in separate places. Gainhelm focuses on the simple dispatch layer: what came in, who is assigned, and what needs to happen next.

Capture pickup requests

Record the client address, items to remove, estimated volume, access notes, and customer context before assigning the truck.

Assign crews clearly

Keep technician assignment, availability, same-day schedule changes, and office-to-field notes visible from one board.

Use mobile-friendly updates

Help office staff and field teams scan the schedule on desktop, tablet, or phone without relying on scattered texts.

Stay focused on dispatch

Gainhelm does not claim estimating, invoicing, payroll, GPS tracking, route optimization, dump fee tracking, salvage resale, or accounting features for this early-access slice.
