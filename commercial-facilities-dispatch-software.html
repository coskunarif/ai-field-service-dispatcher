Commercial facilities dispatch

Commercial facilities dispatch software for service teams.

Gainhelm helps commercial facilities maintenance teams turn service requests into assigned jobs faster: capture the request, see who can take it, update the schedule, and keep the office and field aligned.

Commercial facilities dispatch software should make technician assignment, same-day schedule changes, visit notes, and follow-up visible without forcing a small service team into a heavy enterprise system. For teams comparing facility maintenance scheduling software, Gainhelm stays focused on request intake, technician assignment, and schedule-change handoffs.

Workflow

Keep maintenance jobs moving from intake to assignment.

Commercial facilities dispatch can break down when requests, technician availability, visit notes, and schedule changes live in separate places. Gainhelm focuses on the simple dispatch layer: what came in, who is assigned, and what needs to happen next.

Capture service requests

Record the service address, request type, priority, access notes, and customer context before assigning the job.

Assign technicians clearly

Keep technician assignment, availability, same-day schedule changes, and office-to-field notes visible from one board.

Use mobile-friendly updates

Help office staff and field teams scan the schedule on desktop, tablet, or phone without relying on scattered texts.

Stay focused on dispatch
