When is Jobber the right choice?

Jobber is a solid fit if you need heavy invoicing automation, deposit processing, built-in client messaging campaigns, and quoting workflows inside a single CRM platform.

Why is Gainhelm better for smaller teams?

Owners who manage dispatching alongside service calls need to assign technicians instantly. Gainhelm strips away complex administrative tabs, keeping your scheduling board front and center.

Can I import my data?

Yes. Gainhelm supports simple spreadsheet import for customers, technician details, and address records.
