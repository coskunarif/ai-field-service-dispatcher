Instead of managing messy visual calendars, Gainhelm runs a headless AI dispatcher. Technicians coordinate jobs entirely via automated text messages, while you supervise real-time logs and business rules from a single dashboard.

Workflow

Coordinate urgent jobs before the handoff gets messy.

Emergency plumbing and restoration work often starts with incomplete information, multiple crews, and rapid schedule changes. Gainhelm focuses on the dispatch layer: who should go, what they need to know, and what happens next.

Intake urgent calls

Capture the service address, problem type, priority, access notes, and whether the job needs plumbing, mitigation, or restoration follow-up.

Restoration crew scheduling software

Restoration crew scheduling software should help the office see urgent water-loss jobs, available crews, equipment follow-up, and field updates before the next handoff breaks.

Assign the right crew

Route burst-pipe, active leak, and water-damage calls to available technicians or crews without burying the office in text threads.

Manage the restoration job

Use restoration job management software context to keep photos, notes, drying checks, equipment follow-up, repair estimates, and customer updates tied to the dispatch plan.

Keep follow-up visible

Track drying checks, repair estimates, return visits, and customer updates from one readable dispatch board.
